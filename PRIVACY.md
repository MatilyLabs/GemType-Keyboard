# GemType privacy policy

GemType is a fork of Indic Keyboard. It has no servers, no ads and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

- Your typing. The keyboard learns words locally; text leaves the phone only in the two cases below.
- Your settings and the words the keyboard has learned.
- Your Gemini API key. It is stored encrypted on the device and is excluded from backups and device transfer.

## What is sent, and to whom

- When you tap GemType: the text you chose.
- While you type in English, if "Check as you type" is on (it is on by default; turn it off in Settings → GemType): the text in the field, after a short pause, to find mistakes. It is never sent from password, email, web address or number fields, or for text in other languages.
- It goes directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key, to get the fix. Nothing is routed through a server run by us.
- Language packs are downloaded from the open dictionary project when you add a language.

## Free-tier keys

Requests made with a free-tier Gemini key fall under Google's free-tier terms, which allow Google to use those requests to improve its products. If you do not want that, use a paid-tier key.
