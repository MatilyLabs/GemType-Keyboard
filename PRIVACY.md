# GemType for Android: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026

GemType is a keyboard for Android with a built-in writing assistant, made by Matily
(support@matily.org). This policy explains what GemType does with your data. In short: GemType has
no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We, the developers, never receive what you type.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your typing.** The keyboard learns your words and suggestions on your phone. Typed text leaves
  the phone only in the cases listed under "What is sent, and to whom".
- **Your Gemini API key.** It is stored encrypted on your phone and is excluded from backups and
  device transfers. It is sent only to Google, with each request you make.
- **Your settings, learned words and clipboard history.** They are stored on your phone only.
- **Contact names (optional).** If you turn on "Suggest contact names" and allow access to your
  contacts, the keyboard reads contact names to suggest them while you type. Names are used on your
  phone only and are never sent anywhere.

## What is sent, and to whom

GemType sends text to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), directly from your
phone and with your own API key, only in these cases:

- **When you tap GemType** to fix, rewrite, convert or translate: the text you chose (the selected
  text, or the text in the field).
- **When you use "Fix with GemType"** from another app's text menu, share text to GemType, or tap the
  "Fix clipboard" Quick Settings tile: that text.
- **While "Check as you type" is on:** the English text in the field you are typing in, after you
  pause, to count mistakes. This feature is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any
  time in Settings › GemType. It never sends text from password, email, web address or number fields,
  or text in other languages.

Nothing is routed through a server run by us. Google processes these requests under its own terms
and privacy policy:

- [Gemini API Additional Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Free-tier keys.** Requests made with a free-tier Gemini key fall under Google's free-tier terms,
which allow Google to use them to improve its products. If you do not want that, use a paid-tier
key.

## Other connections

- **Language packs.** When you add a language that needs a dictionary, GemType downloads it from
  GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address as part of that download. No typed text is sent.
- **Problem reports.** "Report a problem" (Settings › About) opens an email in your own email app,
  addressed to support@matily.org, with the app version, Android version and phone model filled in.
  Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you and
  fix the problem.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data or anything you type.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services in GemType.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | Sending your requests to Google's Gemini API and downloading language packs |
| Contacts (optional, asked first) | Suggesting contact names while you type, on your phone only |
| Notifications | Showing download progress for language packs |
| User dictionary | Reading and adding your own words |
| Vibrate | Key press feedback |
| Run at startup | Keeping the keyboard set up correctly after the phone restarts |

## Children

GemType is not directed at children. Google's Gemini API terms require users to be 18 or older.

## Deleting your data

Everything GemType keeps is on your phone. Remove your API key in Settings › GemType, clear learned
words in Settings › Privacy, or uninstall GemType to delete all of it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
